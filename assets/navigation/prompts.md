# Services navigation photography

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. These illustrative agency scenes are
used as decorative previews in the Services menu; they are not portfolio or
client case-study photography.

Optimized assets: `public/navigation/services-v1/` (960 × 600 WebP).

The complete prompt for each image is the shared specification followed by its
scene description below.

## Shared specification

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: service photograph for a boutique web and digital marketing studio's navigation menu.
Primary request: a completely believable candid editorial photograph of real agency work, telling a small human story through an ordinary working moment.
Style/medium: unposed documentary photography, natural camera image, restrained real-world editorial style, normal color balance and exposure. Real materials, a little ordinary wear, small incidental imperfections, convincing everyday proportions.
Composition/framing: horizontal 8:5 photograph; an observational medium close-up from a plausible human viewpoint, with the service's key action clear at small thumbnail size; preserve the central subject within the middle 80% of the image.
Lighting/mood: available window light in an ordinary small creative studio, soft natural shadows, calm and focused, authentic rather than promotional. Warm neutral room, charcoal devices, muted navy or violet details only where naturally appropriate.
Camera: ordinary 35mm documentary photograph, f/4 to f/5.6, moderate depth of field, believable restrained color and contrast, no artificial cinematic bloom.
Constraints: a single real scene; every object rests on a surface or is physically held; correct real device and human anatomy; screens have credible ordinary interfaces fitted inside their screen bezels. Small incidental text may be soft from camera angle, not invented giant text. No watermarks, captions or BellOps logo.
Avoid: 3D render, CGI, illustration, floating objects, glowing objects, neon light, futuristic interfaces, artificial violet lighting, perfectly arranged symmetrical props, showroom surfaces, exaggerated blur, hyperglossy skin, stock-photo smiles, people looking at the camera.
```
