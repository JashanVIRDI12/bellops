### What affects the price of a 3D website

Two sites can look alike in a screenshot and still be very different jobs. These are the things that actually move a quote.

#### 1. Scope: how many pages, and how many of them are 3D

Page count matters, but the number of **3D scenes** matters more. A 3D scene has to be modelled, lit, animated, tested on phones and given a lighter fallback. A standard page doesn't. That's why "one 3D page plus standard pages" (from ₹75,000) costs less than a full 3D website (₹1 lakh to ₹1.5 lakh), even if both have the same number of pages.

#### 2. The number and complexity of 3D models

Models are often the biggest single variable.

- **Licensed models** from trusted 3D marketplaces are included in our prices. We optimise their textures and lighting for the web.
- **Your own CAD files or existing models** can usually be converted and compressed. How messy the source files are affects the effort.
- **Custom models made from scratch** start from ₹5,000 per model. A simple object sits near the bottom of that range. A detailed product with many parts, fine materials or moving pieces costs more.

Model count multiplies quickly. Ten product variants can mean ten models, or one model with ten material options, and the second approach is far cheaper. We'll tell you which one fits during discovery.

#### 3. Interactions and animation

A product that turns slowly as you scroll is one level of work. Click-to-explore hotspots, drag-to-rotate, scroll-linked camera moves and page transitions each add design, build and testing time. Not every brand needs a full 3D world. Sometimes a few micro-interactions and one interactive element in the right place do the job for less.

#### 4. A 3D product configurator

A configurator lets customers rotate a product and switch colours, materials or finishes in real time. It starts from ₹1 lakh because it is really a small application. Every option needs its own materials or model parts, the interface has to stay simple, and the logic has to stay correct as options combine. If it also needs live pricing, saved configurations or a link to your CRM or store, the scope grows. The number of options and how they depend on each other is what pushes the **3D product configurator cost** up or down.

#### 5. Mobile performance work

This is the part cheap 3D sites skip. Most visitors in India arrive on a phone, often on mobile data. A Three.js scene that runs well on a designer's laptop can stutter on a mid-range Android phone and drain its battery. We compress models and textures, load scenes only when they're needed, render the content before the 3D and ship a lighter version of every scene for slower phones, while watching Core Web Vitals. This work is built into our prices, but it's a real part of what you pay for, and it's why a proper **Three.js website costs** more than a template with a 3D embed.

For how slow many Indian D2C storefronts already feel on a budget-phone lab profile (even without 3D), see our companion study: [Indian D2C website speed study](/blog/indian-d2c-website-speed-study).

#### 6. CMS, integrations and languages

Whether your team needs to edit content, and how, changes the build. The same goes for extra languages and integrations such as a CRM, ecommerce or forms. Confirm during discovery which admin setup is included in the starting price for your project.

#### 7. Content and copywriting

Final copy, brand assets and product photos or CAD files ready at kick-off make a project cheaper and faster. If we need to write the copy, plan the page structure from scratch or source product references, that's extra scope.

#### 8. Ongoing care

A website isn't finished at launch. Hosting, security updates, backups and small edits are covered by our [website care plans](/website-care-plans), which start from ₹3,000/month. Plan for this when you budget for the full first year, not only the build.

### How to save on a 3D website without making it worse

1. **Put 3D on one page only.** Usually that's the home page. Build everything else as fast standard pages.
2. **Start with licensed models.** They're included in our prices. Pay for custom models only where your product needs to be exact.
3. **Use one model with material options** instead of a separate model for every variant.
4. **Add 3D to your existing site** (from ₹30,000) if your current site works and only needs a stand-out section.
5. **Have your content ready.** Final copy, brand assets and CAD files at kick-off save time, and time is cost.
6. **Pick one decision-maker.** Fewer rounds of conflicting feedback means a shorter project.

### Bellops vs DIY or a template: an honest comparison

**When DIY or a template makes sense.** If you need a simple site quickly, have a tight budget and don't need custom 3D, a website builder or a template is a reasonable choice. Many builders now let you embed a 3D viewer. For a personal project or an early idea you're still testing, that's often enough, and we'll say so if you ask us. Our [standard landing pages](/landing-pages) start from ₹20,000 if you need something in between.

**Where DIY or templates tend to fall short with 3D:**

- **Performance.** Embedded 3D often loads the full scene up front, with no lighter version for slower phones. On Indian mobile networks, that can mean a slow first load.
- **SEO.** If key content only exists inside a canvas, search engines may not read it. We render the content first and add the 3D on top.
- **Fit.** A template's 3D moment wasn't designed around your product or your customers' questions.
- **Ownership of the details.** Compression, fallbacks and device testing are the unglamorous parts, and they're the ones that decide whether the site holds up.
